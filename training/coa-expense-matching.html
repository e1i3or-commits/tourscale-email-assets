Welcome aboard, Daysi. This guide teaches the two things you'll use every day in Zoho Books: our Chart of Accounts and expense matching. Every account, note, and example below is pulled from our live Books data, so it matches what you'll see on screen. The Common tag marks accounts we used a lot so far this year; a small N× shows roughly how many times year-to-date.

1. The big picture

Two things describe every transaction: the account (what kind of cost it is) and the reporting tags (who it belongs to — entity, business unit, location, department). You set both on almost every expense.

Why tags matter
We run one Zoho Books org for several related LLCs. The account says "Vessel Fuel"; the tags say "this belongs to Trolley Pub of NC, CT Wilmington, Wilmington NC, Operations." Wrong tags silently break every per-brand and per-entity report. See §4.

Rule of thumb
Directly tied to running a tour or producing an asset we sell → COGS (5xxx). Keeps the business running regardless of tour volume → operating expense (6xxx).

How the four move together
Entity, Business Unit and Location almost always travel as a set for a given unit — e.g. the Wilmington tiki boats are always Trolley Pub of NC · CT Wilmington · Wilmington, NC. Departments is the separate "function" lens: Operations (running tours), Franchising (selling/supporting franchises), Supply (manufacturing/TourCraft), or Ad Fund. HQ overhead is TourScale Enterprises · TourScale Enterprises · TourScale HQ + the department it supports. Practice these in §7 Part B.
Tags before save — every time
Entity, Business Unit, Location, and Department must all be set. An untagged expense silently breaks our per-brand, per-market, and per-LLC reports used for tax and partner reporting.

5. Expense matching — the daily workflow

Expense matching = taking a charge from a bank/credit-card feed (Ramp, Bank of America, Chase, Stripe) and tying it to the right account + tags so the books reconcile.

  1. Open the feed (Banking → the account). New charges show as "Uncategorized."
  2. Read merchant + amount + memo — that tells you the account. (The quiz in §7 is exactly this skill.)
  3. Check for a suggested match. If a bill/expense already exists, confirm the green Match. Only categorize when there's no existing record.
  4. Pick the account (§3). Match the merchant the way it was done last time.
  5. Set all 4 mandatory tags.
  6. Attach the receipt (Ramp usually carries it over).
  7. Save / Confirm.
Paid-through accounts
Each LLC/market has its own bank or card — pick the one the money actually moved through: e.g. 1030 Ad Fund - Ramp TRSY, 1034 Supply - Ramp TSE, 1043 Franchising Treasury, 1049 TSE Operating - BOA, or a market account like 1017 Daytona Beach, 1023 PP Madison.

6. Gotchas & house rules

  • Header accounts are grey above — never post to them. Always pick a specific child line.
  • Never use "DO NOT USE" accounts (e.g. 6094). Use Banking Service Fees / Other Banking Fees instead.
  • "Uncategorized" (6999-9) is a parking spot, not a destination. Can't classify it? Leave it there and flag Kai — goal is a zero balance at month-end.
  • "Itemized" is not a real account. It means the expense was split into line items — categorize the individual lines.
  • Loan / credit-card payments: only the interest is an expense (8011/8012); the principal pays down a liability. Split the payment.
  • Tour fuel vs trip fuel: fuel for a boat on tours = Vessel Fuel (5022, COGS); gas for a vehicle on a business trip = Fuel (6358).
  • Software vs services: a tool/subscription is Software (6400s); a person doing the work is a service account (e.g. Bookkeeping Services).
  • Watch the Ops / FranDev / Franchisee split on advertising.
  • Spelling is locked in Books ("Acccounting Software", "Hotels & Accomodations") — match it; don't make new accounts.
  • Don't create accounts or tag options without Kai — the structure is shared across every LLC and reused in tax filings.

8. First-week practice checklist

  • Recite the number ranges from memory (1xxx assets … 8xxx other).
  • Skim §3 and find the five accounts you think you'll use most; learn their notes.
  • Memorize the live tag set in §4 — and remember the dropdown is full of retired options you must not pick.
  • Score 100% on both parts of the §7 quiz (accounts and tags).
  • Categorize 10 real transactions end-to-end (account + all 4 tags + receipt), then have them reviewed.
  • Find one "Itemized" expense and inspect its line items.
  • Pull a P&L by Business Unit and a P&L by Entity to see what your tagging produces.
  • List anything still in 6999-9 Uncategorized and propose a home for each.
Questions on any account or tag — ask Kai before guessing. Consistency with how a vendor was categorized last time is almost always the right call.
